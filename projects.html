AI Coding Tutor

AI Coding Tutor is the Final Individual Project of my Bachelors Degree. It is clear that the beginners of programming are facing many difficulties in the early stage. In Early 2000’s there was no ChatGPT or any AI Tool. So those students tried those stuff by themselves and improved their knowledge.
